# Ciel Ivy-Lee Baumann

I am interested in understanding the fundamental principles underlying nervous systems. I enjoy
inventing new ways of analysing and understanding existing data, ensuring these methods are robust, 
and applying them to neuroscientific data. I am particularly interested in evolutionary and 
developmental neuroscience, form-function relationships, structural bioinformatics, and network 
science. I am also a proponent of reproducible and FAIR+CARE-conscious science.
